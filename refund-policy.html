Refund Policy

Last Updated: February 2026

Simaroa subscriptions are processed by Paddle.com as Merchant of Record.

1. Free Trial

  • Simaroa may offer a 14-day free trial.
  • No payment is required during the trial period.
  • You may cancel at any time before the trial ends to avoid charges.

2. Right of Cancellation (Initial Subscription)

If you are eligible under applicable consumer protection laws, you may request a refund within 14 days of your initial subscription purchase.

  • Refund requests must be submitted through Paddle's support system.
  • If approved under applicable law, refunds will be issued using the original payment method.

3. Subscription Renewals

  • Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled.
  • Except where required by applicable law, renewal payments are non-refundable once processed.
  • You may cancel at any time before the next billing date to avoid further charges.

4. How to Request a Refund

Refunds are handled by Paddle as Merchant of Record. You may request a refund via Paddle's support portal.

This policy does not affect any mandatory rights you may have under applicable consumer law.
